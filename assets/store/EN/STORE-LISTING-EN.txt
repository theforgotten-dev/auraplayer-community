Aura-Player

============================================================
DESCRIPTION
============================================================
Aura-Player brings your local music and videos together in one player for Windows.

Play popular audio formats such as MP3, FLAC, WAV, AAC, M4A and Opus, and video containers such as MP4, MKV, AVI and WebM.

Load SRT, ASS, SSA and VTT subtitles, switch between available subtitle tracks and customize your playback preferences. Aura can also detect associated subtitle files alongside your video and in Subs or Subtitles folders.

Enjoy fullscreen video with floating controls that automatically hide during playback and reappear when you move the mouse. Open files through the file picker or drag and drop them into the player.

Key features:
• Local audio and video playback.
• Live spectrum visualization for supported audio playback.
• Subtitle loading, detection and track selection.
• Play, pause, seek and volume controls.
• Fullscreen playback with floating controls.
• Saved playback and interface preferences.
• Drag-and-drop file opening.

Keyboard shortcuts:
• F11, Alt+Enter or double-click the video: toggle fullscreen.
• Esc: close preferences or exit fullscreen.
• Ctrl+P: open preferences.

Compatibility depends on each file's codecs and characteristics. Aura-Player does not include movies, music or subtitles, and does not decrypt DRM-protected content.
